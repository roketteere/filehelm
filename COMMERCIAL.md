> **TL;DR — if your use is non-commercial, you don't need this. Just
> follow `LICENSE`. If you (or anyone paying you) makes money from
> work that touches FileHelm, you need a commercial license — read
> on.**

FileHelm is dual-licensed:

- **Free** for non-commercial use under
  [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](./LICENSE).
- **Paid** for commercial use under the terms in this document.

This document explains the commercial side. It is **not** a
contract on its own — actual terms are signed after we agree on
scope and pricing.

## Does my use need a commercial license?

You need one if **any** of these are true:

- You use FileHelm at a for-profit company (any size).
- You use FileHelm on a contract / client engagement where you are
  paid for the work.
- You bundle, redistribute, or host FileHelm — modified or not —
  as part of a paid product or service.
- You use FileHelm to develop, build, ship, or operate paid
  software, services, or content.

You do **not** need one if:

- You're a hobbyist using FileHelm for personal projects.
- You're a student or academic using FileHelm for coursework or
  research without a commercial application.
- You're a 501(c)(3) charity, government agency, public school, or
  similar non-commercial organization (per the PolyForm
  Noncommercial "Noncommercial Organizations" clause).
- You're evaluating FileHelm — the first **30 days** of use at a
  commercial entity counts as evaluation; talk to us before day 31
  if you want to keep going.

If you're unsure, ask. We will not chase honest evaluation.

## Pricing

Pricing is currently quote-based — we ask about team size, use
case, and whether redistribution is involved, then send a written
offer. The default shape:

- **Per-seat annual** — covers one human developer using FileHelm
  on for-profit work, billed yearly.
- **Team / organization annual** — covers unlimited internal
  developers at one entity, billed yearly.
- **Perpetual** — one-time fee, covers a specific named version
  forever; doesn't include future major versions.
- **OEM / redistribution** — separate quote; required if you
  bundle FileHelm with your own product.

Educational discounts and start-up rates (pre-revenue, < 5 people)
are available; just ask.

## Bug bounty / contributor terms

If you contribute code via a Pull Request and we merge it, you
agree to license that contribution to us under the same dual
arrangement (PolyForm Noncommercial + Commercial). This lets us
keep selling commercial licenses without requiring co-author sign
off on every customer deal.

## What we do not promise

- We do not promise that the public PolyForm Noncommercial license
  will exist forever. We could change FileHelm's open-side terms
  in future versions. **Once a version has shipped under PolyForm
  Noncommercial, that version stays under PolyForm Noncommercial
  — you're safe to use it forever on those terms.**
- We do not promise support response times unless a commercial
  agreement specifies them.
- We do not promise feature requests will be prioritized; the
  commercial license fee covers the right to use the software, not
  a roadmap commitment.

## Why this license model

We picked **dual-licensing with a non-commercial default** so:

- Individual developers, students, researchers, and small open
  projects pay nothing and don't have to ask permission.
- Companies that build their business on FileHelm pay something
  toward keeping it maintained.
- Forking, modifying, redistributing the source is all permitted
  on the non-commercial side; we're not trying to lock anyone
  out of learning from or improving the code.
